# Privacy Policy for Gemini Ultimate Reader

**Effective Date:** January 22, 2026

## Extension Description
Gemini Ultimate Reader is a browser extension that provides a clean reading mode for Google Gemini (gemini.google.com), featuring theme switching (Light, Sepia, Dark, System) and auto-expansion of source citations.

## Data Collection
**This extension does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

We do not:
- Collect any user information
- Track browsing behavior
- Store any personal data on external servers
- Share data with third parties
- Use cookies or analytics
- Require user accounts or authentication

## Permissions
This extension only uses the following browser permissions:
- `storage`: To save your theme preference locally on your device
- `host_permissions`: To access gemini.google.com for content script injection

All theme preferences are stored in your browser's local storage and are never transmitted anywhere.

## Third-Party Services
This extension does not connect to any third-party services, APIs, or external servers.
